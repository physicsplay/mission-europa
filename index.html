How a playtest works

Break it properly.

A playtest is not “I liked it”. It is what happened, why it matters and what you would change. Your notes decide what gets fixed, and the fix is credited to your crew.

  1. 5 minBriefing

    This week’s mission and your twin crew’s latest post.

  2. 20 minPlay one stage

    In groups of two or three. Write down every moment you got stuck.

  3. 10 minReport

    One mission report with your numbers, one feedback card with your idea.

  4. 10 minPost and reply

    Paste both into TwinSpace, then answer your twin crew.

      The physics menu
      
          
      Pick the physics. Real missions included.
      
          
      Studios vote from a short menu that teachers have checked. Three examples:
      
        
      
        
      
          
      
            Inverse-square law
            
      P ∝ 1 / r²
      
            
      Why does Europa Clipper need solar arrays about 30 m wide?
      
            
            
            
      
              
      3.7%of the sunlight at Earth
      
              
      ×27panel area for the same power
      
            
      
            
      Earth-sized panel
      
          
      
          
      
            Kepler’s third law
            
      T² ∝ a³
      
            
      Io, Europa and Ganymede orbit Jupiter in a 1 : 2 : 4 rhythm.
      
            
            
      Io 0 · Europa 0 · Ganymede 0
      
            
            
      Double the period and the orbit is 22/3 ≈ 1.59 times wider.
      
          
      
          
      
            Momentum transfer
            
      Δv = β · m u / M
      
            
      In 2022, NASA’s DART hit Dimorphos and shortened its orbit by about 33 minutes.
      
            
            
      Orbit around Didymos: 11 h 55 min → 11 h 22 min
      
            
      The missing number is M, the asteroid’s mass. ESA’s Hera arrives in November 2026 to measure it. Once M is known, the push factor β follows.
      
            
      m ≈ 580 kg, u ≈ 6.1 km/s. β > 1 means the flying debris added extra push.

      The decision room
      
        
      One school.
      One vote.
      
        
      New players in every school test version A and version B blind. Each school’s result counts once, whatever the size of the class. The version preferred in most schools goes forward; a tie is decided by the learning data (smaller errors, fewer hints). Then the crews vote on which features of the other version to carry over, so no studio’s work is lost.

          Five rules.
          No exceptions.
          
        
          
        
            
          
          1. Call signs, not names. Your real name never appears on this site or in the games.
            2. 
          
          2. Critique the game, never the person.
            3. 
          
          3. Evidence beats opinion. Say what happened, then what you would change.
            4. 
          
          4. Share only what your school allows. Photos, voices and faces follow your school’s consent rules.
            5. 
          
          5. Your work, your credit. Student contributions are published under a CC BY licence with your crew’s name.
            6. 
        
          
        
          This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no accounts. The games keep your unlocked stages only in your own browser.
